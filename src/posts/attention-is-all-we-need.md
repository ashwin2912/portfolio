When was the last time you made a stand for something YOU believe in?

This attention economy has given the term ‘ nothing comes for free ‘ a whole new meaning. The age of social media has taken away our ability to think independently. All we do is react, we do not think or more importantly - there is no room for thinking. We can think about conspiracy theories (or can we) all day long but I want to outline what I already know to be fact.

Technology is supposed to be an equalizer and is supposed to democratize access to basic needs, and while it has the potential to do that - our current business models rely heavily on the ability to capture and use data in some form or another and in-order to do that - ‘they’ need our attention. I’m not singling out any particular organization or individual. I understand that everything evolves, change is the only constant and evolution is still the best way to maintain equilibrium.

More influential and learned individuals than me have tried to blow this whistle and educate us, but we’ve been reduced to a civilization that treats these warnings as more content and something to feed our brains rather than something to think about.

There’s always room for improvement, there’s always hope - but what happens when there’s now an intelligent assistant whose primary objective is to keep us coming back. It reinforces our wildest thoughts and validates almost everything we say.

All this has gotten me thinking:

> "Is Hallucination a feature and not a bug ?"

> "Is it a coincidence or foreshadowing that the ML paradigm driving the current shift was introduced in a paper called ‘Attention is all you need ‘ ?"

PS: The movie ‘ Don’t look up ‘ - wasn’t talking about the asteroid heading for earth.

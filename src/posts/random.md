There are other realms we don’t understand. AI will help us explore these realms by freeing up time and effort to do things that we don’t want to do. The collective human effort has been in the production of goods and services. The search for meaning and our place in the universe is a very personal endeavor and we need to free up our time and mind-space to be able to dive deeper into the infinite possibilities.

We might not be happy with what we find, but that doesn’t mean we should stop looking.

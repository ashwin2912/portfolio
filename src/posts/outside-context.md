Energy, the most fundamental thing we understand in the universe. It evolved into various different forms, we ‘Homo sapiens’ are one of them. Yuval Noah Harari in his book Sapiens, stated that we are the ‘dominant’ species on this planet because of our ability to communicate which allowed us to collaborate. In his most recent speech, he argues that AI now has the ability to become the universal language that can help us communicate and collaborate more efficiently. He says this can be good and bad, good if we are in control, bad if we are not. 

The collective is always stronger than the one / The collective is the one. 

Let’s look at how AI has changed the way we work right now, I’ve been trying to talk to people from different walks of life to understand their perspectives around the matter. Starting with computer science, my uncle who started his career engineering ECG machines 30+ years ago was telling me his perspective on this topic. What used to take him 6+ months to scope a problem, plan a solution and then plan execution now is done in under 6 days. In some cases, the whole thing is built in 6 days. He offered me an analogy to think about this - if we are walking and see something in our path, we have maybe 3 seconds to decide and alter our course. If we were running, that becomes 1.5 seconds. If we are on a bike, it becomes shorter. If we are on a rocket ship, even shorter. The pilot needs to be able to train their minds to work in the age of AI.
